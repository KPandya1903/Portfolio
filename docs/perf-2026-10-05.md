## Findings

1. **LCP is a simulation artifact, not a regression.** Observed LCP (the hero paragraph) paints at 876 ms, earlier
   than live's 917 ms. The simulated 5.1 s comes from Lighthouse's throttled-network model, run against a local
   `serve` (no CDN, no HTTP/2) for the new site and Vercel's CDN for the live one. **Re-measure on the Vercel preview
   before judging** (Task 16).
2. **TBT +140 ms is the guide.** The 3D chunk spends ~800 ms scripting under 4× CPU throttling and loads inside
   Lighthouse's window (idle callback, 2 s timeout). Still under the 200 ms "good" line. Option if needed: start the
   guide chunk on `load` + 2 s instead of first idle.
3. **The accessibility drop is the intro's hero dimming.** Lighthouse audited while the hero was at 15% opacity and
   flagged `color-contrast` on the name, title and location. It is a 4-second, deliberate state, but it is real low
   contrast for those 4 seconds. **Decision for Kunj:** keep it (score 92), dim less (e.g. 40%, still fails
   contrast), or blur the hero instead of fading it.
4. `target-size` (the carousel's 8 px dots) fails on both sites. It predates this project.
5. Guide chunk 268.6 KB versus a 260 KB budget: three.js core is most of it. Not worth chasing until the real
   avatar's numbers are in.
